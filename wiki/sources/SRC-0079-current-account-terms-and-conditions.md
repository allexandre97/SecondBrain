# Nationwide Current Account Terms and Conditions

## Summary

This is the umbrella agreement governing Nationwide Building Society current accounts, versioned "From 1 May 2025" (document code P4410). It sits at the top of a hierarchy: the Product Terms for a specific account, any overdraft agreement, and any other agreed terms are read alongside it. It covers contacting each other, complaints, joint accounts, keeping the account safe, changes to the agreement, closing the account, payments in and out, what happens when something goes wrong, stopping payments, and borrowing (arranged/unarranged overdrafts and set-off). [SRC-0079]

## Key Points

- The account is for personal use only and cannot be used for business. [SRC-0079, p. 4]
- Nationwide is a mutual; opening an account makes the holder a member, and membership can be withdrawn on unarranged overdrafts. [SRC-0079, p. 31]
- Account holders may be required to join The Nationwide Foundation charity via the charitable-assignment scheme. [SRC-0079, p. 32]
- Closure notice: Nationwide can close an account at any time with two months' notice, or sooner for specific reasons (serious/repeated breach, unpaid debt, fraud, misleading application information, abuse, or legal/regulatory requirements). [SRC-0079, p. 12]
- Unauthorised payments are refundable (usually by the next working day) within 13 months of the payment, with carve-outs for fraud, permission, and gross negligence. [SRC-0079, pp. 24–25]
- Scam ("authorised push payment") refunds apply to qualifying UK bank transfers made on/after 7 October 2024, subject to regulator-set maximums and carelessness carve-outs. [SRC-0079, pp. 24–25]
- Set-off lets Nationwide take money from any of your accounts to repay debts across the group, with at least 14 days' notice and a protection for essential living costs. [SRC-0079, p. 30]
- Overdrafts: arranged overdrafts need an application and agreement; unarranged overdrafts attract no interest but must be repaid promptly. [SRC-0079, pp. 29–30]
- FSCS protection is referenced but detailed in a separate sheet. [SRC-0079, p. 23]
