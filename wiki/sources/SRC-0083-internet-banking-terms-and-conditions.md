# Nationwide Internet Banking Terms and Conditions

Source ID: `SRC-0083`

## Raw source

## Summary

This document (code P3282, October 2023) contains the terms and conditions for using the Nationwide Internet Banking service. It forms a legal agreement read alongside the account terms, and covers definitions (Security Data, Card Reader, etc.), access, the Card Reader, keeping Security Data safe, acting on instructions, joint accounts, MoneyWatch, paperless documents, charges, suspension/termination, changes, and personal data. [SRC-0083]

## Key Points

- Internet Banking is accessed via nationwide.co.uk with a Customer Number, Passnumber, and other Security Data (OTPs, card-reader passcodes, or biometrics). [SRC-0083, paras. 1.1, 2.2]
- Access may be blocked after repeated incorrect Security Data entry; re-registration is required to obtain a new Passnumber. [SRC-0083, para. 2.4]
- You must keep Security Data secret and report any suspected compromise immediately (email customer.service@nationwide.co.uk or phone). [SRC-0083, para. 4.1]
- Nationwide will act on instructions authenticated with your Security Data; it may refuse instructions that breach terms. [SRC-0083, paras. 6.2–6.3]
- No charge for Internet Banking at present, but a charge can be introduced with two months' notice; other transaction charges may apply per account terms. [SRC-0083, paras. 10.1–10.2]
- You can cancel within 14 days; Nationwide may de-register you after 3 months of non-use following registration or 13 months of inactivity. [SRC-0083, paras. 11.2–11.3]
- Changes: two months' notice for charge introductions, 30 days for other terms changes (or sooner where impractical), and immediate for non-material layout/feature changes. [SRC-0083, paras. 12.7]
- Paperless documents are sent via Internet Banking and cannot be changed once sent. [SRC-0083, para. 9]

## Ingestion QA

### Retrieval questions checked

- What is Security Data? The login/authentication details including Customer Number, Passnumber, passcodes, OTPs, and biometrics. [SRC-0083, para. 1.1]
- What must you do if Security Data is compromised? Tell Nationwide immediately and stop use until replacement. [SRC-0083, para. 4]
- Is there a charge? Not currently, but a charge can be introduced on notice. [SRC-0083, para. 10]
- When can access be blocked or de-registered? After repeated wrong entries, or after inactivity (3 months post-registration / 13 months), or for device threats. [SRC-0083, paras. 2.4, 11.3]
- What are the change-notice rules? Two months for charges, 30 days for terms changes, immediate for non-material changes. [SRC-0083, para. 12.7]
