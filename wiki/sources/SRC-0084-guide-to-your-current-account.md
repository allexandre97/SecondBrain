## Summary

This is the general guide to Nationwide current accounts for FlexPlus, FlexDirect, FlexAccount, and FlexBasic (document code P4471, October 2025). It is a practical, non-contractual companion covering contact details, authentication, debit-card use, PIN management, account management, payments, overdrafts, and money-worry support. [SRC-0084]

## Key Points

- Authentication methods: six-digit passnumber, device biometrics, account biometrics, and the card reader. [SRC-0084, p. 4]
- Debit card: usable for goods/services and cash withdrawals; no activation needed for online payments; contactless requires first PIN use and is capped at £100 per transaction. [SRC-0084, pp. 5–6]
- PIN locks after three incorrect entries; unlock at any LINK cash machine; PIN reminders via internet bank. [SRC-0084, p. 6]
- Lost/stolen cards can be reported in the app, internet bank, or on 0800 055 66 22; cards can be frozen/unfrozen in the app. [SRC-0084, p. 8]
- Cash withdrawals: up to £500/day at Nationwide, VISA/LINK cash machines, branches, or the Post Office. [SRC-0084, p. 11]
- Arranged overdrafts apply to FlexPlus, FlexDirect, and FlexAccount (subject to application); unarranged overdrafts attract no interest but must be repaid. [SRC-0084, p. 14]
- Alerts are sent automatically for suspected fraud and insufficient funds, with optional balance alerts. [SRC-0084, p. 10]
- Money-worry support lists independent debt advisers (Money Wellness, StepChange, PayPlan, Citizens Advice, Advice NI). [SRC-0084, p. 15]
