# Nationwide Current Account Switch Service

Source ID: `SRC-0082`

## Summary

This document (code P2205, May 2026) describes switching a current account to Nationwide using the UK Current Account Switch Service. It covers the full and partial switch, the seven-working-day timeline, the Switch Guarantee, the Current Account Switch Agreement, the Account Closure Instruction, and the Switch Data Privacy Notice. [SRC-0082]

## Key Points

- A full switch closes the old account, moves Direct Debits and standing orders, redirects incoming payments (e.g. salary), moves any remaining balance, and brings across saved payees — in seven working days. [SRC-0082, pp. 3–4]
- A full switch does not automatically update card-linked subscription payments, digital wallets, or transfer an old-provider overdraft. [SRC-0082, p. 3]
- You can choose the switch date up to 28 days into the future; the switch process begins 6 working days before the Switch Date. [SRC-0082, pp. 3, 10]
- The redirection service runs for 3 years after the Switch Date, automatically extendable if payments are still being redirected in the final 13 months. [SRC-0082, paras. 1.13–1.14]
- A partial switch moves outgoing payments (up to 20 working days) but keeps the old account open and does not move balance or redirect incoming payments; the Guarantee and Closure Instruction do not apply. [SRC-0082, p. 5]
- The Switch Guarantee refunds interest and charges if anything goes wrong, once reported. [SRC-0082, p. 8]
- You have a 14-day right to cancel the Agreement, with different consequences depending on timing relative to the Switch Date. [SRC-0082, pp. 16–17]
- Third-party-provider permissions are not transferred automatically. [SRC-0082, p. 7]

## Ingestion QA

### Retrieval questions checked

- How long does a full switch take? Seven working days. [SRC-0082, p. 4]
- What does a full switch move? Payments, balance, and payees, and it closes the old account. [SRC-0082, p. 3]
- What does a full switch not do? Update card-linked subscriptions/wallets or transfer an overdraft. [SRC-0082, p. 3]
- How long does payment redirection last? Three years, extendable. [SRC-0082, paras. 1.13–1.14]
- What is a partial switch? Moves outgoing payments but keeps the old account and does not redirect incoming payments. [SRC-0082, p. 5]
- Is there a cancellation right? A 14-day cancellation window with timing-dependent effects. [SRC-0082, pp. 16–17]
