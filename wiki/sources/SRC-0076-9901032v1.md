# On the Reaction Field for Interaction Site Models of Polar Systems

Source ID: `SRC-0076`

## Raw source

- Repository path: `raw/sources/SRC-0076-9901032v1.pdf`
- Open raw source: [raw/sources/SRC-0076-9901032v1.pdf](../../raw/sources/SRC-0076-9901032v1.pdf)

## Summary

Omelyan analyzes reaction-field truncation for rigid interaction-site models of polar liquids. The paper shows that the commonly used fluctuation formula is matched to an individual-site cutoff (ISRF), not automatically to a molecular center-of-mass cutoff with the usual point-dipole reaction field (PDRF). It derives the site-site ISRF potential and a wavevector-dependent dielectric fluctuation formula, then proposes a molecular reaction field (MRF) with quadrupole corrections. [SRC-0076, abstract and §§2–3]

The central implementation warning is that finite molecular size must be represented consistently in both the truncated interaction and reaction-field correction. In an MCY water test, PDRF produced nonphysical oscillations and deviations of roughly 25% in wavevector-dependent dielectric quantities near the first Kirkwood-factor maximum, while ISRF gave smoother results. [SRC-0076, §4]

## Key points

- The microscopic field can be written using charge density or polarization density; for interaction-site models the full polarization operator must be retained before taking a point-dipole limit. [SRC-0076, §§2–3]
- Pure spherical cutoff converges to the infinite-system field as $R^{-1}$, while reaction-field treatment converges as $R^{-2}$ for nonzero wavevectors. [SRC-0076, §2]
- ISRF applies site-site truncation and produces a quadratic charge-separation term in the potential. [SRC-0076, eqs. 9–11]
- Standard molecular cutoff plus PDRF is not self-consistent for finite molecules because it combines site-site Coulomb interactions with a point-dipole reaction field. [SRC-0076, §§1–2]
- MRF adds quadrupole–quadrupole and quadrupole–dipole terms. Standard PDRF error scales as $d/R$, while modified MRF is expected to reduce it to $(d/R)^2$. [SRC-0076, §3]
- A complete fluctuation formula for molecular-cutoff MRF is left for future work. [SRC-0076, §3]

## Methods and evidence

The theoretical development uses Fourier transforms of charge and polarization fields, longitudinal wavevector components, spherical Bessel functions, first-order response to an external field, and inverse transformation to obtain real-space potentials. The numerical demonstration uses Monte Carlo simulations of the MCY water model with $N=256$, density $1.0\ \mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}$, $T=292\ \mathrm{K}$, and $R=9.856\ \mathring{\mathrm{A}}$. [SRC-0076, §§2 and 4]

ISRF and PDRF agree at large wavevector, where boundary effects are negligible, but differ substantially at intermediate wavevector. The largest reported deviation is about 25% near $k=3\ \mathring{\mathrm{A}}^{-1}$, close to the first maximum of the Kirkwood factor. [SRC-0076, §4]

## Mathematical structure

The derivation proceeds as follows: define microscopic charge/polarization fields; derive Fourier-space SC and RF fields; use longitudinal dielectric response and first-order perturbation theory to eliminate the external field; derive the ISRF real-space potential by inverse transform; expand the conducting-cavity molecular reaction field in dipole, quadrupole, and higher multipoles; symmetrize the molecular interaction energy; and compare finite-size scalings. [SRC-0076, §§2–3]

## Key equations

The wavevector-dependent ISRF fluctuation formula is: [SRC-0076, eq. 7]

$$
\frac{\varepsilon_L(k)-1}{\varepsilon_L(k)}
=
\frac{9yG_L(k)}{1+27yG_L(k)\,\frac{j_1(kR)}{kR}}
=9y g_L(k),
\qquad
y=\frac{4\pi N\mu^2}{9Vk_BT}.
$$

Here $G_L(k)$ is the finite-system longitudinal Kirkwood factor and $g_L(k)$ its infinite-system limit. [SRC-0076, eq. 7]

The site-site ISRF potential is: [SRC-0076, eq. 11]

$$
\phi_{ij}^{ab}=
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle
\frac{q_aq_b}{|r_i^a-r_j^b|}
\left(1+\frac{|r_i^a-r_j^b|^2}{2R^3}-\frac{3}{2R}\right), & |r_i^a-r_j^b|\le R,\\
0, & |r_i^a-r_j^b|>R.
\end{cases}
$$

The MRF potential retains multipole corrections through quadrupole terms: [SRC-0076, eq. 16]

$$
\phi_{ij}=\sum_{a,b}\frac{q_aq_b}{|r_i^a-r_j^b|}
-\frac{\mu_i\cdot\mu_j}{R^3}
-\frac{q_i:q_j-3(q_i:\mu_j r_{ij}+q_j:\mu_i r_{ji})}{6R^5},
\qquad r_{ij}\le R_d.
$$

## Variable glossary

- $N$, $V$, $T$: molecule count, sample volume, and temperature.
- $q_a$, $r_i^a$: charge and position of site $a$ on molecule $i$.
- $\mu_i$: permanent molecular dipole; $\mu=|\mu_i|$.
- $R$: site-site cutoff radius; $R_d=R-d/2$ is the molecular cutoff.
- $d$: molecular diameter; $k$: wavevector magnitude; $j_0$, $j_1$: spherical Bessel functions.
- $G_L(k)$, $g_L(k)$: finite-system and infinite-system longitudinal Kirkwood factors.
- $\varepsilon_L(k)$: longitudinal wavevector-dependent dielectric constant.
- $q_i$: molecular quadrupole tensor; $W(R_d)$: weighted quadrupole contribution to MRF.

## Proof map

The paper contains formal derivations rather than theorem proofs. Its logical chain is: exact charge/polarization field representation → Fourier-space truncation fields → linear-response elimination of the applied field → inverse-transform ISRF potential → conducting-cavity multipole expansion for MRF → finite-size error comparison → MCY numerical comparison. [SRC-0076, §§2–4]

## Implementation notes

- Match cutoff operator, reaction field, and polarization observable at the same spatial resolution: site-site with ISRF, or molecular with multipole-aware MRF.
- Do not apply the standard point-dipole fluctuation formula to a molecular center-of-mass cutoff without checking missing finite-molecule terms.
- For wavevector-dependent dielectric analysis, evaluate the longitudinal component and retain $j_1(kR)/(kR)$.
- Treat the MRF formula as incomplete for dielectric extraction until a compatible molecular-cutoff fluctuation estimator is derived.

## Mathematical gaps

- The paper does not derive the complete fluctuation formula corresponding to the molecular-cutoff MRF potential.
- Higher multipoles are truncated after quadrupole-level correction; omitted terms are argued to be order $(d/R)^3$.
- Numerical evidence is one MCY-water state with 256 molecules and does not establish behavior across water models, densities, temperatures, or flexible/polarizable molecules.

## Ingestion QA

### Retrieval questions checked

- What is the central contribution? It distinguishes self-consistent site-site RF from molecular-cutoff RF for interaction-site models and proposes ISRF/MRF remedies. [SRC-0076]
- Why is standard PDRF problematic? It combines site-site Coulomb interactions with a point-dipole reaction field, ignoring finite molecular size. [SRC-0076]
- What is the ISRF fluctuation formula? Equation 7 relates $\varepsilon_L(k)$ to $G_L(k)$, $g_L(k)$, and the finite-$R$ Bessel correction. [SRC-0076]
- What corrections does MRF add? Quadrupole–quadrupole and quadrupole–dipole terms, with higher multipoles omitted. [SRC-0076]
- How do truncation errors scale? Pure SC is $R^{-1}$, RF is $R^{-2}$ for the field, and standard molecular PDRF introduces error of order $d/R$. [SRC-0076]
- What evidence supports ISRF? MCY-water PDRF oscillations are absent or reduced under ISRF, with deviations reaching about 25% near $k=3\ \mathring{\mathrm{A}}^{-1}$. [SRC-0076]
- What remains unresolved? A complete molecular-cutoff MRF fluctuation formula. [SRC-0076]
- What should not be overgeneralized? The rigid, nonpolarizable, single-state MCY-water test. [SRC-0076]
