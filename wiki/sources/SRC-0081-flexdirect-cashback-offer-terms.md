# Nationwide FlexDirect Cashback Offer Terms

Source ID: `SRC-0081`

## Raw source

- Repository path: `raw/sources/SRC-0081-flexdirect-cashback-offer-terms.pdf`
- Open raw source: [raw/sources/SRC-0081-flexdirect-cashback-offer-terms.pdf](../../raw/sources/SRC-0081-flexdirect-cashback-offer-terms.pdf)

## Summary

These offer terms describe the FlexDirect Cashback offer that started on 25 September 2024 (document code P4327, September 2024). They are separate from the FlexDirect account terms and cover eligibility, qualifying spend, cashback rate and cap, and how and when cashback is paid. [SRC-0081]

## Key Points

- Cashback: 1% on qualifying spend, capped at £5 per month, for 12 months from account opening. [SRC-0081, paras. 2, 7]
- Eligibility requires opening FlexDirect on/after 25 September 2024 and paying in at least £1,000/month (excluding transfers from other Nationwide accounts and Visa credits). [SRC-0081, para. 7]
- Qualifying spend is payments made with the FlexDirect debit card, including contactless, Apple/Google/Samsung Pay, and recurring card payments. [SRC-0081, para. 3]
- Excluded transactions: gambling, cryptocurrency (including crypto exchanges), cash withdrawals, other cash-type transactions (foreign currency, travellers cheques, money orders, loan/lease/mortgage payments), and non-card payments (transfers, standing orders, direct debits). [SRC-0081, para. 12]
- You cannot opt out of the offer if you qualify, and previous FlexDirect holders (or those auto-converted from FlexGraduate/FlexOne before 1 January 2025) do not qualify. [SRC-0081, paras. 9–11]
- Nationwide can change, replace, or withdraw the offer on 30 days' notice for funding exhaustion, technical, or legal/regulatory reasons. [SRC-0081, para. 5]
- Cashback is rounded to the nearest pence, paid on the last day of the month, and appears as "Everyday Spend Cashback" on the statement. [SRC-0081, paras. 14–15]
- The monthly calculation cut-off is 8.30pm on the last day of the month (or Saturday if the last day is a Sunday). [SRC-0081, para. 18]

## Ingestion QA

### Retrieval questions checked

- What is the cashback rate and cap? 1% up to £5/month for 12 months. [SRC-0081, paras. 2, 7]
- Who qualifies? New FlexDirect accounts opened on/after 25 September 2024 paying in £1,000/month. [SRC-0081, para. 7]
- What spend is excluded? Gambling, crypto, cash withdrawals, cash-type transactions, and non-card payments. [SRC-0081, para. 12]
- When and how is cashback paid? Rounded to the nearest pence, paid monthly on the last day, shown as "Everyday Spend Cashback". [SRC-0081, paras. 14–15]
- Can the offer be withdrawn? Yes, on 30 days' notice for stated reasons. [SRC-0081, para. 5]
