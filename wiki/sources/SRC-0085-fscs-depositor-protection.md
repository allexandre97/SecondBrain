# Nationwide FSCS Depositor Protection Information Sheet

## Summary

This two-page information sheet (document code P2957, April 2026) explains how deposits with Nationwide Building Society and Virgin Money are protected by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS), including the protection limit, temporary high balances, joint accounts, how payment is made, and an exclusions list. [SRC-0085]

## Key Points

- FSCS protects eligible deposits up to £120,000 per depositor per bank/building society/credit union. [SRC-0085, p. 1]
- Nationwide and Virgin Money balances are aggregated for the £120,000 limit. [SRC-0085, p. 1]
- Joint accounts: each eligible holder gets £120,000 (e.g. £240,000 total for two holders); business-account deposits are treated as a single depositor. [SRC-0085, p. 1]
- Temporary high balances (home purchase/sale, major life events, compensation awards) can exceed £120,000 protection for six months. [SRC-0085, p. 1]
- FSCS typically returns deposits within seven business days, by cheque or electronic transfer. [SRC-0085, p. 1]
- Exclusions include credit/financial institutions, investment/insurance/reinsurance undertakings, collective investments, pension/retirement funds, public authorities, deposits not held at a UK (or Gibraltar) establishment, and deposits linked to money-laundering convictions. [SRC-0085, p. 2]
- Contacts: FSCS at fscs.org.uk, 0800 678 1100, or enquiries@FSCS.org.uk; Nationwide at 03457 30 20 11. [SRC-0085, p. 1]

## Ingestion QA

### Retrieval questions checked

- What is the protection limit? £120,000 per depositor per institution. [SRC-0085, p. 1]
- Are Nationwide and Virgin Money combined? Yes, aggregated for the limit. [SRC-0085, p. 1]
- How are joint accounts treated? Each holder gets £120,000. [SRC-0085, p. 1]
- What are temporary high balances? Event-linked balances that can exceed the limit for six months. [SRC-0085, p. 1]
- How quickly does FSCS pay? Typically within seven business days. [SRC-0085, p. 1]
- What is excluded? Institutional holders, non-UK/Gibraltar deposits, and money-laundering-linked deposits. [SRC-0085, p. 2]
