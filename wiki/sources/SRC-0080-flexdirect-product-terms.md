# Nationwide FlexDirect Product Terms

## Summary

These are the product terms for the Nationwide FlexDirect current account (document code P4413, May 2025). They form part of the agreement alongside the Current Account Terms and Conditions, and take precedence where the two differ. The document covers the monthly fee, self-service account management, interest paid on balances, foreign-currency card payments, and payment charges (CHAPS/SWIFT). [SRC-0080]

## Key Points

- No monthly fee; FlexDirect is a self-service account managed via Digital Banking or by phone rather than in branch. [SRC-0080, p. 1]
- Introductory interest: 5% AER (4.89% gross) fixed for the first 12 months on balances up to £1,500. [SRC-0080, p. 1]
- Standard interest after 12 months: 1% AER (0.99% gross) variable, still capped at £1,500; no interest above £1,500. [SRC-0080, p. 1]
- Interest requires paying in at least £1,000/month, excluding transfers from other Nationwide accounts and card refunds. [SRC-0080, p. 2]
- The introductory rate is unavailable if you have held a FlexDirect account before (individual); joint accounts get the introductory rate again with a different joint holder. [SRC-0080, p. 2]
- Foreign-currency fees: 2.99% of the payment amount for card payments, cash withdrawals in a foreign currency in the UK, and cash withdrawals abroad (the UK fee does not apply at a LINK cash machine or over the counter). [SRC-0080, p. 2]
- Payment charges: £15 for CHAPS (large UK value payments) and £15 for SWIFT international/non-GBP payments (no charge for Euro payments to the UK or EEA). [SRC-0080, p. 3]
