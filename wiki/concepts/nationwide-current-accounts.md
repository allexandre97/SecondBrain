# Nationwide Current Accounts

## Summary

Nationwide Building Society (a UK mutual owned by its members) offers a family of personal current accounts. This page anchors the bundle of Nationwide banking documents ingested as `nationwide-current-accounts`: the umbrella terms and conditions, product-specific terms for FlexDirect, a FlexDirect cashback offer, the Current Account Switch Service, Internet Banking terms, a general account guide, and the FSCS depositor-protection information sheet. [SRC-0079] [SRC-0080] [SRC-0081] [SRC-0082] [SRC-0083] [SRC-0084] [SRC-0085]

## Account family

- **FlexDirect** — self-service current account with an introductory fixed interest rate on balances up to £1,500 and a cashback offer. [SRC-0080] [SRC-0081]
- **FlexPlus, FlexAccount, FlexBasic** — the other current-account variants covered by the account guide; FlexBasic is the no-chequebook option. [SRC-0084]
- **FlexOne** — youth account (ages 11–17) referenced in the terms and conditions. [SRC-0079]
- **FlexGraduate** — referenced only as a source account that can auto-convert to FlexDirect. [SRC-0081]

## How the documents fit together

The umbrella agreement is the Current Account Terms and Conditions. [SRC-0079] Product Terms for a specific account sit alongside it and take precedence where they conflict. [SRC-0080] The cashback offer is a separate offer layered on top of the FlexDirect account. [SRC-0081] The switch service, Internet Banking terms, account guide, and FSCS sheet are auxiliary documents covering account movement, digital access, day-to-day use, and depositor protection respectively. [SRC-0082] [SRC-0083] [SRC-0084] [SRC-0085]

## Key Points

- Nationwide is a mutual: opening an account makes the holder a member with membership rights, and unarranged overdrafts can affect membership. [SRC-0079]
- FlexDirect interest is fixed at 5% AER (4.89% gross) for the first 12 months up to £1,500, then a variable 1% AER (0.99% gross) standard rate, contingent on paying in at least £1,000/month. [SRC-0080]
- Deposits are FSCS-protected up to £120,000 per depositor, with a higher limit possible for temporary high balances and joint accounts treated separately. [SRC-0085]
- The Current Account Switch Service performs a full switch in seven working days under a guarantee; the redirection agreement runs for three years (extendable). [SRC-0082]
